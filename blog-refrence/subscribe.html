Where the money goes

Roughly three quarters of subscription revenue pays reporters, editors and the freelance photography budget. The rest covers hosting, legal review for investigations, and the access-request fees that a story like the grid rebuild runs up over eleven months.

We publish the full breakdown once a year, audited, alongside the number of subscribers. Both figures go up and down; we print them either way.

Before you subscribe

Can I cancel whenever I want?
Yes, from account settings, in two clicks. Your access runs to the end of the period you have paid for, and we do not email you to ask why.
What happens to my saved articles if I cancel?
They stay in your account. You keep the list and can export it as a file; you simply lose access to the pieces that sit behind the paywall.
Do you offer a student rate?
£3 a month, on the honour system. Select it at checkout — there is nothing to upload and nobody checks.
Is there a team or institutional rate?
Yes, for newsrooms, universities and libraries. Contact the newsroom and we will work out something proportionate to your size.
Will you sell my data?
No. We do not run third-party advertising trackers, and reader data is never sold or shared for marketing. The privacy notice lists every processor we use.
